In short

External insulation in a few lines

In short, Danova Costruzioni prices: External wall insulation from €63/m² (Italian VAT at 10% included). The exact price comes with the free written quote, itemised and fixed in the contract.

External wall insulation (in Italian cappotto termico) is an insulation system applied to the outside of walls: insulating boards glued and anchored, reinforcing mesh, base coat and coloured finish. It cuts heat loss in winter and overheating in summer, removes many thermal bridges and much mould, and renews the look of the building. In 2026 it qualifies for the Italian ecobonus, with a deduction of 50% on the main residence and 36% on other properties, if it meets the technical requirements.

The stages

How we install insulation properly

  1. Assessment and designWall check, insulation thickness calculation and details: sills, window reveals, balconies, plinth.
  2. PermitsBuilding permit, public land occupation if needed, ecobonus documentation and ENEA filing.
  3. Scaffolding and preparationScaffold erection, cleaning and consolidation of the substrate, starter profiles.
  4. Board installationGluing and anchoring to the manufacturer’s pattern, with special care at corners and window junctions.
  5. Base coat and finishReinforcing mesh, double base coat, silicone or acrylic finish in the chosen colour.
  6. HandoverScaffold removal, cleaning, documentation and system warranties.

FAQ

Questions about external insulation.

How much does external insulation save?

It depends on the starting building, thickness and heating system. On uninsulated houses from the 1960s to 80s the saving on heating is usually significant; the initial assessment gives you an estimate for your case.

What deduction applies in 2026?

External insulation qualifies for the ecobonus: in 2026 the deduction is 50% for the main residence and 36% for other properties, meeting the thermal requirements and paperwork. From 2027 the rates fall to 36% and 30%.

Does the apartment building need to approve it?

Yes, for shared facades a resolution of the owners’ meeting is needed. We prepare design, renders and quote for the meeting.

Does insulation cause mould indoors?

On the contrary: when properly designed and installed it raises the temperature of interior walls and reduces the condensation that causes mould. Details around windows matter a lot.

Can you insulate from the inside?

Yes, when the facade cannot be changed (for example in a listed historic centre) you can insulate from inside with insulated drywall linings.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We come to the property, measure, check systems and structures and give you a written, itemised quote. You pay nothing and commit to nothing.

What does fixed price mean?

The price written in the contract is the final price for the work described. If during the job you decide to add or change something, we quote it in writing first: no extra item appears on the invoice without your approval.

How do you guarantee deadlines?

Start date, completion date and intermediate stages are written into the contract, together with the work schedule. Crews and materials are planned before the site opens, so the calendar does not depend on last-minute availability.
