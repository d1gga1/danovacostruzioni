FAQ

Questions about custom furniture.

Is custom furniture much more expensive than off-the-shelf?

Not always: for irregular spaces, niches and unusual heights custom furniture uses space better and avoids adaptations. The quote lists every element with materials and finishes.

Can I use the Italian furniture bonus?

If you are renovating a home with work that qualifies for the renovation bonus, in 2026 you can also deduct furniture and appliances, with a €5,000 spending cap. We explain how to document the purchase.

How long does it take?

It depends on quantity and materials; we write the installation date into the contract.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We come to the property, measure, check systems and structures and give you a written, itemised quote. You pay nothing and commit to nothing.

What does fixed price mean?

The price written in the contract is the final price for the work described. If during the job you decide to add or change something, we quote it in writing first: no extra item appears on the invoice without your approval.

Tell us about the space to furnish.

A photo and rough measurements are enough to start: we survey and make a first proposal.
