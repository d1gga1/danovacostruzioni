Conditions

What “from” means

  1. Prices are indicative and not binding and apply to work in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia. Price list updated on 5 November 2026: each request is priced on the list published on the date of the quote.
  2. Each “from” price refers to the work described in the “What is included” column, with the minimum areas, site condition and standard materials stated. Smaller areas, premium materials, harder conditions or extra work lead to a different price, which we state in writing before you sign.
  3. Prices include Italian VAT at 10%, the reduced rate for work on private homes. For companies, non-residential buildings or other cases set by law the applicable rate is charged.
  4. Unless stated, not included: design, site management and building permits (CILA, SCIA, permits) with their fees; scaffolding and public land occupation; disposal of asbestos or special waste; structural work; furniture, kitchens and appliances; special transport, for example by boat in Venice.
  5. The binding price is only the one in the written quote, given after the free survey, and in the signed contract. From then on the price of the agreed items is fixed.
  6. An extra cost is possible only for changes requested by the client or for problems not visible at the survey (for example hidden pipes or decayed beams): in that case we give you the cost in writing and proceed only after your approval.
  7. The information on this page is not an offer to the public under art. 1336 of the Italian Civil Code. Tax deductions depend on your personal situation and the rules in force.
FAQ

Questions about prices? Answers

Are the “from” prices what I will pay?

They are the starting price for the work described in the “What is included” column. If your job matches that description, the price starts there. If premium materials, smaller areas or extra work are needed, the quote states it item by item before you sign. The price that counts is the one in the written quote and contract, and then it stays fixed.

Why are you cheaper than average?

Because we work with one point of contact and one coordinated team, buy materials directly from suppliers and plan the site with dates written in the contract. Fewer hand-offs and fewer days on site mean lower costs.

Is VAT included?

Yes, at 10%, the reduced Italian rate for work on private homes. For companies and non-residential buildings the rate set by law applies.

Can there be extra costs during the work?

Not on the items in the contract: that price is fixed. An extra cost is possible only if you ask for a change or a problem appears that was not visible at the survey, such as hidden pipes or decayed beams. In that case we give you the cost in writing and proceed only after your approval.

Do I pay for the survey?

No, the survey and quote are free and with no obligation across Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia, usually within a week of your request.

Can I get an estimate without a survey?

Yes: send us 3 or 4 photos and rough measurements on WhatsApp and we will tell you which range the job falls into. The exact price comes with the written quote after the survey.
